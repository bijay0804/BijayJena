ABOUT PROJECT

Why StudyMate?

Students often manage assignments, practicals, examinations and study sessions using different notebooks or applications. This can make it difficult to remember deadlines and monitor academic progress.

StudyMate provides one simple platform where students can plan their studies, manage tasks and track completion.
